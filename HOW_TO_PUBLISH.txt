HOW TO PUBLISH YOUR EPORTFOLIO (free, GitHub Pages)

1. Sign in to GitHub and create a new public repository, for example "cet257-eportfolio".
2. Upload everything in this folder: index.html, the assets folder, the evidence folder
   (including evidence/pdf) and the .nojekyll file.
   Tip: on the repository page choose Add file > Upload files and drag the whole contents in.
3. Open Settings > Pages. Set Source to "Deploy from a branch", choose the main branch and
   the root folder, then Save.
4. After a minute your link will be https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/cet257-eportfolio/
5. Open the link and test it on your phone as well as your computer.
6. Paste the link on the title page of your C1 report, the C2 portfolio and the C3 reflections,
   and into Canvas.

Before you publish, check these three things:
- The "Try the prototype" buttons use https://shilustha71-arch.github.io/teamspeed-prototype/
  (the link in your email of 12 September, E6.6). Open it and confirm it still works.
- The footer shows your university email address. Open index.html in a text editor and
  search for "bwit25.slc@ismt.edu.np" if you want to change or remove it.
- File names are case-sensitive on GitHub Pages. Keep the evidence file names exactly as they are.

You can also open index.html directly on your computer to preview the site.
The Word evidence files in evidence/ are the originals. The PDFs in evidence/pdf/ were
converted from them so that visitors can read them in the browser without downloading.
